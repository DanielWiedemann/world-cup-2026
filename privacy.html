World Cup Schedule — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

World Cup Schedule is a free app that shows the FIFA World Cup 2026 fixtures in your local timezone and, optionally, sends you push notifications about matches you've opted into. This policy describes exactly what data the app handles.

What we collect

The app collects information only when you choose to enable notifications. If you never tap the bell button, no personal data leaves your device.

  • Push subscription token — when you enable notifications, your browser generates a unique URL issued by your device's push service (Google FCM on Android, Mozilla autopush on Firefox, Apple APNs on iOS). We store this URL so we can deliver notifications to your device. It does not contain your name, email, phone number, or contacts.
  • Notification preferences — the four toggle settings you choose: pre-game, kickoff, goal, full-time.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, browsing history, or any analytics or advertising identifiers.

How we use it

The push subscription token and your preferences are used only to deliver the notifications you asked for. Match data itself is fetched publicly from ESPN's open scoreboard endpoint and is not tied to you in any way.

Where it's stored

Subscriptions and preferences are stored on Cloudflare Workers KV. Push delivery is routed through your device vendor's push service (Google, Mozilla, or Apple), which is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Retention & deletion

Your subscription is deleted in any of these cases:

  • You tap "Turn off all" in the app's notification settings.
  • You uninstall the app or clear browser data — the next time the push service reports the subscription as gone, we delete it automatically.

Third parties

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party. The only services involved in delivering notifications are the push service built into your device (Google FCM, Mozilla autopush, or Apple APNs).

Children

World Cup Schedule does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no user accounts.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, where the change is meaningful, surface it in the app.
